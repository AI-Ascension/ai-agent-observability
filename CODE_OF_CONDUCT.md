# Project Conduct

We collaborate directly, respectfully, and in good faith. Reviews should
challenge code, configuration, and reasoning with evidence while treating
contributors with patience and dignity.

Expected behavior includes welcoming useful questions, stating assumptions,
accepting correction, giving actionable review feedback, respecting privacy and
consent, and taking responsibility for mistakes. Unacceptable behavior
includes harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, deliberate
intimidation, disclosure of private information, malicious disruption, or
retaliation for a good-faith report.

This policy applies in repositories, issue trackers, review threads, chat,
release spaces, and public situations where someone represents the project.
Report conduct concerns through the private channel described in
[`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).
